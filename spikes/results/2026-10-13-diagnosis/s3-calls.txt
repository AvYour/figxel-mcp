OUT: # Motion — keyframe animation on the timeline

Motion is animation along a frame's timeline (Figma's Motion beta). It is not a prototype transition between frames (topic "prototype"). Figma enables it per user [C-115]: when a motion tool answers UNSUPPORTED, stop — tell the user and deliver the static design; do not retry, and do not fake it with prototype transitions.

## Rules
- Animate the layers INSIDE a top-level frame, never the top-level frame itself: the frame owns the timeline its children play on.
- Manual keyframes (figma_set_keyframes) are the default. Animation styles show in figma_get_motion_context but cannot be applied through figma-mcp yet.
- Times are seconds. Values are strings by field: a number "0.5" (OPACITY, ROTATION, TRANSLATION_X …), a vector "10,20" (TRANSLATION_XY, SCALE_XY), a colour "#ff000080" ("fill:<i>", "stroke:<i>", an effect's COLOR). Only a SOLID paint animates.
- Easing names are Figma's: EASE_IN_AND_OUT, not EASE_IN_OUT. Also LINEAR, EASE_IN, EASE_OUT, the _BACK forms, GENTLE, QUICK, BOUNCY, SLOW, HOLD (a jump), "cubic:x1,y1,x2,y2" and "spring:<bounce 0-1>". A keyframe's easing shapes the move from the previous keyframe into it.
