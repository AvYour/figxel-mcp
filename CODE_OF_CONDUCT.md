# Code of Conduct

Figxel adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**, as its code of conduct. The full text is at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html>, and it applies here as if it were written out in this file.

In short: we want taking part in Figxel to be a harassment-free experience for everyone. Be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, accept feedback on your work gracefully, and focus on what is best for the project and the people in it. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualised language or imagery, and publishing someone else's private information without their permission are not acceptable. The full text is the binding version.

## Scope

The code of conduct applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, reviews, commits, and any other channel the project uses) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers privately, through the e-mail address on a maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not report it in a public issue. Reports are handled privately, and the privacy and safety of the person reporting are respected.

Security vulnerabilities are a different channel: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainers are responsible for enforcing the code of conduct. They follow the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines, which scale from a private correction through a warning and a temporary ban to a permanent ban, depending on the impact of the behaviour.
